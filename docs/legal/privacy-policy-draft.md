## Information processed

Depending on how you use Vioscribe, the service processes:

- account email and sign-in identifiers; authentication is provided by Supabase, and Google identity information is also used if you choose Google sign-in;
- a generated display name, friend code, account preferences, and daily-goal and streak values;
- notes, decks, flashcards, and filing choices you create;
- friend requests and accepted connections;
- study reviews, timer sessions, study-room membership and temporary room presence;
- technical request, diagnostic, and security information that the hosting and authentication providers may process.

Vioscribe does not ask for a date of birth and does not currently have an age gate. Do not include sensitive personal information in notes, decks, or cards.

## Why information is used and proposed lawful bases

The service uses account and study information to authenticate you and provide the study features you request. The proposed basis is that processing is necessary to perform the service agreement with you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b), where applicable). Limited processing to secure, maintain, and prevent misuse of the service is proposed to rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). Processing required by law relies on the relevant legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). The operator must confirm these bases and document any required legitimate-interests assessment before publication.

Vioscribe does not use AI to process study content, sell personal information, run behavioural advertising, or use analytics tracking.

## Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides authentication, database, and real-time services. Netlify hosts the website and may process technical request and diagnostic information. Google is involved if you choose Google sign-in. These providers may use subprocessors under their terms and privacy documentation. Their roles, data locations, and transfer safeguards must be confirmed in the readiness checklist before publication.

Your private notes, cards, and decks are for your account. Friend features expose your generated display name and relevant activity to people connected to you. Room participants can see your display name and presence while you are in a room. If you enable a deck share link, anyone with that link can view the shared deck content.

## Cookies and device storage

Vioscribe uses authentication cookies needed to maintain a secure signed-in session. The landing-page intro no longer stores a persistent preference on your device. Vioscribe does not use analytics or advertising cookies. This inventory must be rechecked if analytics, embeds, or other third-party scripts are added.

## Storage and retention

Account and study records are kept while your account is active. You can request account deletion by contacting us. The database schema links account data for deletion when an account is removed; the operator must confirm that account removal is operationally available and verify provider backup and security-log retention periods. Provider backups or logs may retain information under their own retention schedules.

The Supabase project region, any international transfers, and the applicable safeguards must be identified before publication. Ask the contact above for details about the safeguards that apply to your information.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access to your information, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the [UK Information Commissioner’s Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
